## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Common Issues

### iPad Not Detected

- Make sure your iPad is unlocked
- Check that the USB cable is firmly connected
- For Wi-Fi, verify both devices are on the same network

### Slow Performance

- Try using USB instead of Wi-Fi for a faster connection
- Close unused apps on your iPad
- Keep your iPad plugged in to avoid battery drain

### Connection Drops

- Move closer to your Wi-Fi router
- Try a different USB cable
- Restart both devices and try again

### App Won't Open

- Ensure your iPad is properly jailbroken
- Reinstall the app using your package installer
- Check that you downloaded the correct version for your iOS

## 🧪 Technical Details for the Curious

ipadScreen is built with native Objective-C for the iPad side and uses Swift with ScreenCaptureKit on the Mac side. The technology stack includes:

- **libjpeg-turbo** with NEON acceleration for fast image compression
- **Theos** for iOS 6 compatibility
- **ScreenCaptureKit** for capturing your Mac's display

This combination ensures the best possible performance even on older hardware.
